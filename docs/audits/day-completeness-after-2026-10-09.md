# Day-shape review — regenerated samples

All 10 trips / 47 days were regenerated through real local authenticated Trailie conversations. The initial fixtures were held unchanged during the audit and candidate review; no canonical content was hand-edited. Follow-ups are recorded in private captures.

## Review decisions

- **Yellowstone National Park**: Restored the actual canyon outing through Trailie; three full sightseeing days are explicit. Northern day reserves substantial approach/return time.
- **Glacier National Park**: Five full sightseeing days explicitly exclude arrival/departure. West-to-east base move is represented. Long lake hike spans morning–afternoon; meals and recovery remain visible.
- **Utah Mighty 5**: All five parks remain included. Arrival/departure and base moves are explicit; longest Bryce/UT-12 day disclosed. Chosen Capitol Reef and Arches outings are complete, without promising omitted alternatives.
- **Alaska National Parks**: Nine overnight bases now match overview. Bus and cruise are full sightseeing experience blocks with unbooked-service caveats, not fake visitor-center pins or mislabeled relocations. Anchorage breaks the long cross-region transfer.
- **Yosemite National Park**: One moderate hike only. Final Village/cultural day and riverside viewpoint no longer depend on Glacier Point reopening. Full sightseeing-day assumption and extra travel for an alternative base are clear.
- **Grand Canyon + Page**: Major rim outing, east-rim transfer to Page, and final rest day have distinct purposes. Four full usable days and departure outside them are explicit; Horseshoe Bend depends on safe heat/daylight.
- **Olympic National Park**: Northern route avoids reported Hoh Bridge/Mora closures. Explicit tradeoff: Sol Duc old-growth forest instead of a Hoh visit; Strait coastline outside the park rather than outer Pacific coast. No closure reopening is assumed.
- **Big Bend National Park**: Exactly two saved days with Friday arrival outside them. Santa Elena/Basin dependencies removed. Saturday recovery protects night photography; Sunday finishes after lunch with no sunset or extra-night promise.
- **Washington’s National Parks**: Empty relocation days replaced by full travel/meals/recovery blocks. Thunder Knob gives North Cascades a concrete moderate main outing. Northern Olympic scope and park-complex versus national-park interior distinction are explicit.
- **Acadia National Park**: Sunrise is relative to date-specific light and access, not an invented clock. Recovery after dawn is explicit. Sunday is a full sightseeing day by assumption, with an optional earlier departure explained.

## Limits and evidence

The batch contains 56 grounded place stops, 253 day-shape blocks, and 2 full service-experience days without fabricated pins. Day types: {"sightseeing":30,"arrival":2,"transfer":10,"departure":4,"rest":1}. Every day passes the structural guard. These checks do not establish that every trail, road, business or service will operate on an unspecified future date.

Most inter-base and approach/return routes have no measured duration. They are represented as travel blocks with generous, explicitly unconfirmed allowances. Only stored verified edges are displayed as measured transfers. Overnight-base checks confirm that moves are accounted for; geographic feasibility without routed evidence remains a review/model judgment.

Editorial review rejected initial incomplete/conditional main outings and one failed Big Bend patch that retained the old saved plan. The normal guard now returns the actual patch failure reason to its correction model, and capture rejects a failed latest mutation or mismatched response identity. Initial attempts remain private audit evidence.

External access cross-checks used [Big Bend current conditions](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/conditions.htm), [Chisos construction FAQ](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/chisos-basin-construction-projects-faq.htm), and [Olympic Hoh Bridge closure notice](https://home.nps.gov/olym/planyourvisit/hwy-101-closure-at-hoh-river-bridge.htm).
