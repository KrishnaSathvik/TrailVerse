## One-paragraph product summary

TrailVerse is a free national-parks exploration and trip-planning platform covering 470+ U.S. National Park Service parks and sites — not only the ~63 headline National Parks, but monuments, historic sites, seashores, recreation areas, and more. Travelers discover parks, compare finalists, check live alerts and weather, and plan day-by-day itineraries with Trailie, an AI trip planner grounded in live park data when available. Most browsing works without an account; sign-in unlocks saved trips, unlimited Trailie planning, and deeper logistics. TrailVerse also reaches users in ChatGPT, Claude (MCP connector), and a native iOS app in active development.

**Short line:** Trailie on TrailVerse plans outdoor trips across 470+ NPS parks and sites — compare parks, build day-by-day itineraries, and factor in permits, closures, weather, and crowds when the data is available.

---

## Problem

Planning a U.S. national-park trip usually means juggling many tools:

- Search “63 national parks” and miss hundreds of other NPS destinations
- Generic AI itineraries from training memory, not today’s closures
- Fragmented stack: NPS.gov for rules, Recreation.gov for bookings, AllTrails for hikes, blogs for vibes — no single discover → compare → plan hub
- Road closures, timed entry, weather, and crowd spikes change after the itinerary is written
- Hard to compare 2–4 parks on weather, crowds, campgrounds, and fit before booking

## Solution

TrailVerse is the planning layer for the full NPS system:

1. Discover — Explore 470+ sites; filter by state, activity, topic, and trip vibe
2. Decide — Side-by-side compare (up to 4 parks); park pages with alerts, weather, permits, campgrounds
3. Plan — Trailie builds and refines day-by-day itineraries with live context when available
4. Book elsewhere — Deep links to Recreation.gov and official NPS sources

**Positioning:** Plan the trip in TrailVerse → book on Recreation.gov → hike with AllTrails → trust NPS.gov for official safety.

## Features

- Explore — Search/filter 470+ NPS sites; “National Parks Only” toggle (~64)
- Park detail — Overview, alerts, things to do, campgrounds, permits, photos, reviews, and more
- Compare — Side-by-side 2–4 parks (weather, crowds, campgrounds, and more)
- Map — Interactive map of parks
- Discover (Explore by Activity) — Browse by activity, type, topic, state
- Trailie — AI chat itineraries; anonymous trial; unlimited + saved trips when signed in
- Voice — Talk to Trailie on most pages
- Crowd Calendar — Seasonal when-to-go guidance
- Planning Guides + vibe lists — Editorial guides and ranked lists (couples, families, dark sky, etc.)
- Blog — Trip planning, park guides, seasonal, astrophotography
- Events — Ranger programs / NPS event listings
- Account — Saved/visited parks, chat history, trip workspace, share links
- ChatGPT app — Live park tools inside ChatGPT
- Claude MCP — Same data tools via connector
- iOS — Native app in progress (discover → compare → plan)

**What TrailVerse is not:** Not Recreation.gov (no issuing reservations). Not AllTrails (no turn-by-turn trail GPS). Not a hotel booking engine. Not “always real-time” for every fact — live data when available. Not an official NPS product.

## User journey

Land (search, social, ChatGPT, Claude) → Explore / Discover / Map → Open park detail (alerts, weather, permits) → Optional: Compare 2–4 finalists → Plan with Trailie → Refine itinerary → Book permits/camps on Recreation.gov → Optional: save trip, share, return via chat history.

**Alternate entries:** Start on Compare, Trailie, vibe landings, or ChatGPT/Claude; continue on the website for saved trips.

**Why sign up:** Save parks, unlimited Trailie, chat history, share links, deeper logistics for named non-NPS destinations.

## Competitor comparison

Complementary stack, not winner-take-all. TrailVerse owns system-wide trip planning; others own booking, official notices, or on-trail GPS.

| Dimension | TrailVerse | AllTrails | Recreation.gov | NPS App / NPS.gov | Generic ChatGPT (no tools) |
|-----------|------------|-----------|----------------|-------------------|----------------------------|
| Primary job | Discover → compare → plan across 470+ NPS sites | Individual trail hikes + GPS | Book camps, timed entry, permits | Official info & alerts | Text from memory |
| Live NPS alerts on park pages | Yes | Not core | Partial / booking-focused | Authoritative source | No |
| Park-to-park compare (2–4) | Yes | No | No | Limited | Unreliable |
| AI itinerary with live park context | Trailie + ChatGPT/Claude tools | No full park-trip planner | No | No | Hallucination risk |
| On-trail GPS / crowd trail reports | No | Core strength | No | Limited | No |
| Issue reservations | No (links out) | No | Yes | No | No |
| Free browse without account | Yes | Free Base; extras paid | Free to use | Free | Free (platform plan) |

**Punchline:** AllTrails for the trail. Recreation.gov for the reservation. NPS for the final word. TrailVerse for which park, when, and how the days fit together.

**Competitor sites:** alltrails.com · recreation.gov · nps.gov · chatgpt.com/apps · thedyrt.com
