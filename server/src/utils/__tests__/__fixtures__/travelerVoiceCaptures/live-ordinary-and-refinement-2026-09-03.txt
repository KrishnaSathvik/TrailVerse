Plan 3 days in Badlands National Park. I like scenic drives and easy walks, nothing strenuous.
1:57 PM

Trailie

I'd shape this as three unhurried, drive-forward days that move from the east side of the park toward the more central and western overlooks. That gives you a different landscape each day without turning easy walks into long hikes. I've left out Castle Trail and Deer Haven: their published distances make them poor fits for a nothing-strenuous trip. Start each morning with the farther stop for that day, then make the overlooks and short accessible walks your flexible pauses. The named sites here are spread out enough that you should check that morning for road and site access before setting out, and simply linger at the views that are looking best rather than trying to rush every stop.

Keep it easy

Door Trailhead and Fossil Exhibit Trailhead are described as easy, but no walking distances were provided for them here. Treat both as choose-your-own-length strolls rather than committing to a set mileage. The overlooks are ideal places to build in breaks, and there is no need to add a long trail to make these days feel full.
