Beyond Trailie, I've been making a lot of smaller improvements throughout TrailVerse to make exploring destinations and planning trips easier.

If you have a trip in mind, even just a rough idea, I'd really love for you to try Trailie and see how it feels.

And if something doesn't work well, or there's something you wish it could do, email me at hello@nationalparksexplorerusa.com. That feedback genuinely helps me figure out what to improve next.
